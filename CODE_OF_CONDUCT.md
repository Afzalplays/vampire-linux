# Code of Conduct

We aim to keep the Vampire OS community welcoming, constructive, and focused
on technical collaboration.

Expected behavior includes respect, evidence-based technical disagreement,
good-faith corrections, and respect for privacy. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive conduct are unacceptable.

Report serious or repeated concerns privately to repository maintainers.
Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when needed.
